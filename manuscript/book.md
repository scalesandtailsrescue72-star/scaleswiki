# Educational Disclaimer

This publication is intended solely for educational purposes.

The information contained within this guide is designed to assist reptile keepers in understanding husbandry, preventive care, enclosure design, nutrition, and general welfare for Ball Pythons (*Python regius*).

This guide is **not** intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.

Veterinary medicine continually evolves. Recommendations may change as new scientific evidence becomes available.

Readers should consult a qualified reptile veterinarian regarding any medical concerns involving their animal.

# About ScalesWiki

ScalesWiki was founded with a simple mission:

To improve reptile welfare through accurate education, practical husbandry guidance, and collaboration with experienced veterinary professionals.

Our goal is to bridge the gap between brief care sheets and advanced veterinary textbooks by creating educational resources that are approachable for beginners while remaining valuable to experienced keepers, educators, and rescue organizations.

Every ScalesWiki publication emphasizes:

• Evidence-informed husbandry

• Preventive care

• Responsible ownership

• Lifelong learning

• Veterinary collaboration

• Animal welfare

Education should never replace professional veterinary medicine.

Instead, education should empower reptile keepers to recognize good husbandry practices, identify potential concerns early, and seek qualified veterinary care when appropriate.

# About Scales & Tails Rescue

Scales & Tails Rescue was established to provide safe placement, rehabilitation, and education for reptiles in need.

Beyond rescue work, we believe education is one of the most powerful tools available to improve reptile welfare.

Every successful adoption begins with knowledgeable husbandry.

Every healthy reptile begins with informed keepers.

The educational resources produced by ScalesWiki are intended to support this mission by making reliable husbandry information accessible to reptile owners around the world.

Our long-term vision includes:

• Educational publications

• Online learning

• Community outreach

• Rescue support

• Veterinary collaboration

• Conservation awareness

# How to Use This Guide

This field guide has been designed for both first-time reptile keepers and experienced hobbyists.

Each chapter follows a consistent format:

📖 Main educational content

❤️ Rescue Insight

💡 Fun Fact

⭐ Pro Keeper Tip

⚠️ Common Mistakes

📋 Keeper's Toolkit

🩺 Veterinarian's Corner

🎨 Chapter Infographic

📚 References

The guide may be read from beginning to end or used as a reference manual for specific husbandry topics.

Readers are encouraged to maintain written records of husbandry practices, veterinary visits, feeding, shedding, and environmental monitoring throughout the life of their animal.
