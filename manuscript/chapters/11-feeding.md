# 11 Feeding

## Opening Quote
> "Nutrition is care, not just calories."

## Introduction
Feed appropriately sized prey items and adjust feeding frequency based on age, body condition, and health status.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand feeding basics for ball pythons.
- Learn how to choose appropriate prey size and frequency.
- Recognize feeding issues that may require veterinary input.

## Main Sections
- Prey size and type
- Feeding frequency
- Appetite and feeding problems

## Veterinary Review Point
Feeding should be adjusted for growth, health, and body condition.

## Rescue Insight
Rescues can sometimes provide useful feeding history and preferences.

## Fun Fact
A healthy ball python may go through occasional fasting periods without being sick.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Observe body condition regularly rather than relying solely on appearance at feeding time.

## Common Mistakes
- Feeding prey that is too large
- Feeding too frequently or too rarely
- Ignoring appetite changes

## Chapter Summary
Feeding should support growth, maintenance, and overall health.
