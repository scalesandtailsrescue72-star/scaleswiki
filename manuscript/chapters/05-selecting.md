# 05 Selecting

## Opening Quote
> "A careful choice is the first act of responsible care."

## Introduction
Choose a healthy animal by observing body condition, alertness, demeanor, and signs of respiratory or skin issues.

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize signs of a healthy ball python.
- Know what questions to ask the breeder or rescue.
- Avoid common selection mistakes.

## Main Sections
- Body condition and behavior
- Health questions to ask
- Red flags to avoid

## Veterinary Review Point
A careful selection process can reduce the risk of hidden illness or chronic husbandry concerns.

## Rescue Insight
Rescue organizations can provide temperament and history details that are extremely useful.

## Fun Fact
A well-selected snake often settles into captivity more quickly and with less stress.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Watch the animal's response to handling and movement before making a decision.

## Common Mistakes
- Focusing on color and pattern over health
- Missing subtle signs of poor condition
- Skipping a health or temperament discussion

## Chapter Summary
Selection is about matching the animal's health and temperament to your ability to provide care.
