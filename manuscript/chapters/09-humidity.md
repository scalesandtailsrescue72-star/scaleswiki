# 09 Humidity

## Opening Quote
> "Humidity should be measured with intention."

## Introduction
Humidity should be monitored and adjusted carefully to support healthy skin, shedding, and respiratory function.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the role of humidity in reptile health.
- Learn how to measure and adjust humidity.
- Recognize signs of humidity-related issues.

## Main Sections
- Humidity ranges
- Measurement tools
- Adjustment strategies

## Veterinary Review Point
Humidity problems can contribute to shedding issues and respiratory stress.

## Rescue Insight
A newly arrived snake may need more predictable humidity until it settles in.

## Fun Fact
Shedding depends heavily on hydration and environmental stability.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Monitor humidity in the enclosure, not just room air, to get a useful reading.

## Common Mistakes
- Ignoring humidity entirely
- Making sudden, extreme changes
- Relying on visual guesswork instead of measurements

## Chapter Summary
Humidity management supports skin, shedding, hydration, and comfort.
