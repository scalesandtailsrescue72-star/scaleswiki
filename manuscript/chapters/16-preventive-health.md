# 16 Preventive Health

## Opening Quote
> "The best medicine is early attention."

## Introduction
Preventive care includes routine observation, sanitation, proper husbandry, and timely veterinary support.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the basics of preventive reptile health care.
- Learn how to monitor for early signs of problems.
- Build a routine that supports long-term wellbeing.

## Main Sections
- Routine observation
- Husbandry-based prevention
- Veterinary support and record keeping

## Veterinary Review Point
Preventive care is one of the most effective ways to reduce serious illness.

## Rescue Insight
Rescue animals often benefit from extra observation during the first months in a new home.

## Fun Fact
Small changes in appetite, body condition, or shedding can reveal significant health concerns.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Maintain simple health records to track trends over time.

## Common Mistakes
- Waiting until the animal appears sick
- Ignoring subtle changes in appetite or shedding
- Skipping routine veterinary conversations

## Chapter Summary
Preventive health care protects the animal before problems become severe.
