# 18 Emergencies

## Opening Quote
> "In an emergency, calm preparation matters."

## Introduction
Seek veterinary care promptly for signs of severe lethargy, difficulty breathing, prolapse, or trauma.

## Learning Objectives
- Know what situations count as emergencies.
- Prepare for rapid response.
- Understand the importance of transportation and veterinary contact.

## Main Sections
- Emergency warning signs
- Immediate response steps
- Transport and veterinary planning

## Veterinary Review Point
Emergency response time can be critical to outcomes.

## Rescue Insight
A rescue animal may arrive with limited history, so emergency preparedness is especially important.

## Fun Fact
Some emergencies are not obvious until the animal becomes very unwell.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Keep a transport plan and contact list ready before an emergency happens.

## Common Mistakes
- Delaying veterinary care
- Handling the animal too much during an emergency
- Not having a transport container or emergency contacts ready

## Chapter Summary
Preparedness helps keepers respond quickly and confidently during a crisis.
