# 12 Water

## Opening Quote
> "Clean water is one of the simplest but most important care tools."

## Introduction
Fresh, clean water should always be available and replaced regularly to support hydration and hygiene.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the importance of water quality.
- Learn how to provide water safely and effectively.
- Recognize dehydration and hydration concerns.

## Main Sections
- Water availability
- Water cleanliness and replacement
- Hydration signs

## Veterinary Review Point
Hydration issues can quickly become serious if overlooked.

## Rescue Insight
A new snake may need careful monitoring of water intake and behavior.

## Fun Fact
Ball pythons often use water for both hydration and comfort during shedding.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Use a dish that is easy to clean and large enough for the snake to soak if it wants to.

## Common Mistakes
- Leaving water dirty or stagnant
- Using a bowl that is too small
- Missing signs of dehydration

## Chapter Summary
Water is essential for hydration, shedding, and basic health.
