# 08 Heating

## Opening Quote
> "Warmth should be controlled, not guessed."

## Introduction
Heating should be provided through a safe, regulated gradient so the snake can choose its preferred temperature.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the importance of thermal gradients.
- Learn how to choose safe heat sources.
- Monitor temperatures effectively.

## Main Sections
- Heat source options
- Thermal gradient setup
- Monitoring and safety

## Veterinary Review Point
Improper heating can cause stress, respiratory problems, or dehydration.

## Rescue Insight
Newly adopted snakes may need a slower transition to a stable thermal setup.

## Fun Fact
Ball pythons use temperature choice to regulate digestion and activity.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Use a thermostat and a reliable thermometer rather than relying on guesswork.

## Common Mistakes
- Using heat sources without thermostat control
- Failing to measure surface and ambient temperatures
- Creating an enclosure that is too hot at one end and too cool at the other

## Chapter Summary
A stable and safe thermal gradient is essential to health and comfort.
