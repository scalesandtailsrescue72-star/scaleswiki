# 07 Enclosure

## Opening Quote
> "The enclosure should feel safe before it feels impressive."

## Introduction
A properly sized enclosure should provide security, climbing opportunities, hides, and appropriate ventilation.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the basics of enclosure design.
- Recognize the importance of hides, ventilation, and security.
- Build a setup that supports normal behavior.

## Main Sections
- Size and dimensions
- Layout and enrichment
- Security and ventilation

## Veterinary Review Point
A well-designed enclosure supports both physical health and mental wellbeing.

## Rescue Insight
Rescue snakes may need a quieter, more secure enclosure during adjustment periods.

## Fun Fact
Ball pythons often spend much of their day hidden and feel safest with multiple secure retreats.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Offer at least two hides, one on each thermal side of the enclosure.

## Common Mistakes
- Choosing an enclosure that is too small
- Overcrowding the habitat with decor
- Ignoring ventilation or escape risk

## Chapter Summary
A good enclosure balances security, functionality, and comfort.
