# 15 Cleaning

## Opening Quote
> "Clean spaces support healthy animals."

## Introduction
Regular cleaning of the enclosure and accessories supports hygiene and reduces odor and pathogen buildup.

## Learning Objectives
- Learn basic enclosure cleaning practices.
- Understand how hygiene supports reptile health.
- Organize a cleaning routine that is safe and practical.

## Main Sections
- Routine cleaning
- Disinfection and drying
- Cleaning schedule planning

## Veterinary Review Point
A clean enclosure reduces exposure to pathogens and supports animal comfort.

## Rescue Insight
A newly arrived snake may need a gentler and more controlled cleaning routine.

## Fun Fact
Many enclosure problems are easier to prevent than to solve later.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Remove waste promptly and clean accessories regularly to reduce odor and contamination.

## Common Mistakes
- Cleaning while the animal is still in the enclosure without care
- Using harsh chemicals or residues
- Neglecting hidden areas or water bowls

## Chapter Summary
Consistent cleaning supports health, comfort, and a safe environment.
