# 10 Lighting

## Opening Quote
> "A consistent routine helps the animal feel secure."

## Introduction
Lighting supports the animal's day-night rhythm, and a consistent photoperiod is generally recommended.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand why lighting matters.
- Learn how to build a simple photoperiod.
- Recognize the difference between light and heat.

## Main Sections
- Light as a cue
- Photoperiod basics
- Safe lighting choices

## Veterinary Review Point
A regular light cycle supports behavior and physiology.

## Rescue Insight
Some rescue snakes need extra time to adjust to a predictable routine.

## Fun Fact
Reptiles use light cues to help regulate activity and rest patterns.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Keep the enclosure lighting consistent rather than changing it unpredictably.

## Common Mistakes
- Confusing heat lamps with full-spectrum day lighting
- Overly bright or irregular light schedules
- Ignoring the importance of darkness and rest

## Chapter Summary
Lighting supports rhythm, behavior, and overall environmental consistency.
