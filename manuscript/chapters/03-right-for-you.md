# 03 Is a Ball Python Right for You?

## Opening Quote
> "A good decision is one made with preparation, not impulse."

## Introduction
Consider your experience, housing setup, budget, and long-term commitment before acquiring a ball python.

## Learning Objectives
- Evaluate readiness for reptile ownership.
- Understand the physical and financial commitments involved.
- Decide whether a ball python fits your household and routine.

## Main Sections
- Time and lifestyle commitment
- Housing and budget planning
- Long-term care expectations

## Veterinary Review Point
A realistic care plan reduces the risk of preventable husbandry and health issues.

## Rescue Insight
Adoption can be a responsible and rewarding way to welcome a ball python into a home.

## Fun Fact
Ball pythons can live for decades with proper care, making long-term planning essential.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Discuss care expectations with every household member before the animal arrives.

## Common Mistakes
- Choosing a snake based on appearance alone
- Underestimating enclosure and equipment costs
- Assuming a reptile is low-maintenance

## Chapter Summary
Responsible ownership begins with honest self-assessment and planning.
