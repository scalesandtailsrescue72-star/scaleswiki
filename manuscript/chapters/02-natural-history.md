# 02 Natural History

## Opening Quote
> "The best keeper understands the animal's wild origins."

## Introduction
Ball pythons are native to West and Central Africa and thrive in warm, humid environments with cover and security.

## Learning Objectives
- Learn the natural habitat and behavior of ball pythons.
- Understand how wild ecology informs captive husbandry.
- Recognize how environmental features influence care.

## Main Sections
- Geographic range and habitat
- Activity patterns and behavior
- Seasonal and environmental influences

## Veterinary Review Point
Wild ecology helps explain why stable temperature, humidity, and security are essential.

## Rescue Insight
Many rescue snakes arrive with incomplete husbandry histories, making background knowledge especially valuable.

## Fun Fact
Ball pythons are constrictors and use body coils to secure prey rather than venom.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Observe how your snake reacts to temperature gradients and hiding spaces in the enclosure.

## Common Mistakes
- Copying husbandry from unrelated species
- Ignoring the impact of humidity on shedding
- Overestimating how much space alone replaces security

## Chapter Summary
Natural history provides the biological context for modern captive care.
