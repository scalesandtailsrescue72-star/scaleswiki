# 14 Handling

## Opening Quote
> "Gentle handling builds trust without forcing comfort."

## Introduction
Handle gently and with purpose, allowing the snake to feel secure while avoiding unnecessary stress.

## Learning Objectives
- Learn safe handling practices.
- Understand how handling affects stress levels.
- Know when to pause or avoid handling.

## Main Sections
- Safe handling basics
- Handling frequency and duration
- Signs the snake needs a break

## Veterinary Review Point
Handling should be adapted to health status, stress levels, and recovery needs.

## Rescue Insight
Some rescue snakes benefit from a slow, low-pressure introduction to handling.

## Fun Fact
A snake that feels secure often handles more calmly and predictably.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Support the body fully and avoid sudden movements or overhandling.

## Common Mistakes
- Grabbing or restraining too quickly
- Handling during shedding or illness
- Assuming the snake wants frequent interaction

## Chapter Summary
Handling should be calm, purposeful, and responsive to the animal's state.
