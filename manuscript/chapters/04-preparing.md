# 04 Preparing

## Opening Quote
> "Preparation prevents stress for both keeper and snake."

## Introduction
Prepare a secure enclosure, suitable heat and humidity, and a feeding and husbandry plan before bringing a snake home.

## Learning Objectives
- Set up the enclosure before arrival.
- Gather essential equipment and supplies.
- Create a safe and stable environment from day one.

## Main Sections
- Enclosure setup
- Equipment checklist
- Arrival-day planning

## Veterinary Review Point
A prepared setup reduces the chance of environmental stress and health problems.

## Rescue Insight
Many rescue snakes benefit from quiet, predictable routines immediately after placement.

## Fun Fact
A properly prepared setup can make the transition home much smoother for a new snake.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Double-check heat sources, thermostat settings, and water availability before the animal arrives.

## Common Mistakes
- Waiting until the last minute to build the enclosure
- Using untested equipment or poor-quality hides
- Forgetting backup heat or humidity plans

## Chapter Summary
Preparation is the first step toward a calm and successful transition.
