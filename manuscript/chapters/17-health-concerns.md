# 17 Health Concerns

## Opening Quote
> "Recognizing a problem early can change everything."

## Introduction
Common concerns include respiratory issues, mites, scale problems, and weight loss or reduced appetite.

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize common health concerns in ball pythons.
- Learn which signs require prompt attention.
- Understand when professional veterinary care is appropriate.

## Main Sections
- Respiratory concerns
- Parasites and external issues
- Weight and appetite changes

## Veterinary Review Point
Many health issues are easier to manage when noticed early.

## Rescue Insight
Rescue snakes may require extra observation because medical history may be incomplete.

## Fun Fact
Subtle changes in behavior or posture can precede more obvious symptoms.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Trust your observations and document anything unusual, even if it seems minor.

## Common Mistakes
- Waiting too long to seek care
- Assuming poor shedding is harmless
- Missing early respiratory signs

## Chapter Summary
Understanding common health concerns helps keepers respond quickly and appropriately.
