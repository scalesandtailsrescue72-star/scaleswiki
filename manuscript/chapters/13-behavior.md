# 13 Behavior

## Opening Quote
> "Behavior reveals the animal's comfort level."

## Introduction
Ball pythons often show stress through defensive postures, refusal to feed, or excessive hiding.

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize normal and abnormal behavior.
- Understand how stress appears in ball pythons.
- Learn how to adjust husbandry based on behavior.

## Main Sections
- Normal behavior patterns
- Signs of stress
- Environmental influences

## Veterinary Review Point
Behavior changes can reflect environmental stress or health problems.

## Rescue Insight
Behavior can take time to normalize after adoption or transport.

## Fun Fact
A calm ball python may still spend long periods concealed, which can be normal.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Track behavior over time rather than reacting to one unusual event.

## Common Mistakes
- Assuming all hiding is a problem
- Ignoring repeated defensive responses
- Treating behavior changes as purely personality-based

## Chapter Summary
Behavior is a useful clue to welfare and husbandry quality.
