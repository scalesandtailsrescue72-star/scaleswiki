# 06 Quarantine

## Opening Quote
> "Isolation is prevention in practice."

## Introduction
Quarantine helps reduce the chance of spreading parasites or infectious disease to other reptiles.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand why quarantine matters.
- Learn how to set up a temporary isolation space.
- Recognize the signs that require veterinary attention during quarantine.

## Main Sections
- Purpose of quarantine
- Setup and monitoring
- When to seek veterinary help

## Veterinary Review Point
Quarantine is a core preventive health strategy for reptiles.

## Rescue Insight
Rescue intake often benefits from a quarantine period before introductions to shared spaces.

## Fun Fact
Even healthy-looking reptiles can carry parasites or pathogens without obvious symptoms.

## Pro Keeper Tip
Keep quarantine equipment separate from the main enclosure setup to prevent cross-contamination.

## Common Mistakes
- Skipping quarantine entirely
- Reusing shared tools without cleaning
- Ending quarantine too early

## Chapter Summary
Quarantine protects the new animal and the broader collection from preventable disease.
