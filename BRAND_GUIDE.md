# 🐍 ScalesWiki Brand Guide
Version 1.0

---

# Mission

> **The Place Where People Learn Reptile Husbandry.**

ScalesWiki exists to provide evidence-based reptile husbandry education that is accessible, engaging, and trustworthy for keepers, rescues, educators, and veterinary professionals.

---

# Vision

To become the world's most trusted educational platform for reptile husbandry.

---

# Brand Promise

Education first.

Sustainability second.

ScalesWiki will always strive to keep its core educational resources freely available while building sustainable ways to support future growth.

---

# Core Values

## 📚 Education

Teach people not only what to do, but why.

---

## 🔬 Evidence

Content should be based on current husbandry knowledge, veterinary guidance, and continual review.

---

## ❤️ Better Care

Better education leads to better reptile welfare.

---

## 🌎 Community

Build relationships between keepers, rescues, educators, veterinary professionals, and responsible industry partners.

---

# Brand Personality

ScalesWiki should always feel:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Trustworthy
- Educational
- Modern
- Welcoming
- Evidence-based

Avoid:

- Fear-based messaging
- Clickbait
- Sensationalism
- Elitism
- Gatekeeping

# ScalesWiki Academy

The Academy expands the educational experience.

Members receive:

- Progress tracking
- Certificates
- Printable guides
- Worksheets
- Advanced quizzes
- Premium educational resources

Core educational guides remain free.

---

# Founders Tree

The Founders Tree recognizes the individuals and organizations who helped ScalesWiki grow during its earliest years.

Each supporter becomes a permanent leaf on the tree.

Organization leaves may link directly to their websites.

Recognition categories include:

- Veterinary Partners
- Rescue Partners
- Education Partners
- Industry Partners
- Founding Members

---

# Sustainability

ScalesWiki is supported through:

- Academy memberships
- Donations
- Affiliate partnerships
- Digital educational products

Every membership helps keep educational content freely available.

---

# The ScalesWiki Standard

Every new feature should answer YES to at least one:

✓ Does this improve reptile welfare?

✓ Does this help people learn reptile husbandry?

✓ Does this strengthen the ScalesWiki community?

✓ Does this support the long-term sustainability of the project?

If the answer is no, reconsider building it.

---

# Closing Statement

ScalesWiki exists because better education creates better reptile keepers.

Every guide, lesson, Snapshot, certificate, partnership, and Academy course should support one mission:

> **The Place Where People Learn Reptile Husbandry.**
