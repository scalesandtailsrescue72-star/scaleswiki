# ScalesWiki Style Guide

## Voice and Tone
- Use clear, confident, and educational language.
- Write for reptile keepers, rescue partners, and curious readers.
- Keep explanations practical, supportive, and evidence-informed.
- Avoid overly casual slang and avoid fear-based framing.

## Writing Style
- Prefer short paragraphs and clear section headings.
- Use direct, active voice whenever possible.
- Define technical terms simply on first use.
- Keep lists scannable and action-oriented.

## Formatting
- Use Markdown headings in a consistent hierarchy.
- Use bold for important terms and emphasis.
- Use bullet points for checklists, tips, and summaries.
- Use blockquotes sparingly for quotes or callout notes.

## Content Principles
- Balance care guidance with welfare and ethics.
- Highlight veterinary awareness without replacing professional advice.
- Include rescue-informed perspectives where relevant.
- Make content useful for both beginners and experienced keepers.

## Terminology
- Use "ball python" as the standard common name.
- Use "Python regius" for the scientific name.
- Prefer "keeper" over "owner" when describing care responsibilities.
- Prefer "enclosure" over "tank" when discussing habitat design.

## Visual and Editorial Notes
- Keep chapter structure consistent across the manuscript.
- Use a professional field-guide tone for published materials.
- Support content with practical checklists, toolkits, and summaries.
- Reserve playful or decorative language for fun facts and callouts.
