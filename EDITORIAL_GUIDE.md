# ScalesWiki Editorial Guide

## Purpose
ScalesWiki content should be clear, trustworthy, educational, and useful for reptile keepers, rescue partners, and readers exploring responsible care.

## Editorial Principles
- Favor accuracy, clarity, and practical value.
- Write in a calm, confident, and supportive tone.
- Use evidence-informed guidance and avoid unsupported claims.
- Keep language accessible without being overly simplified.

## Content Standards
- Use consistent chapter structure across the manuscript.
- Introduce technical terms clearly when first used.
- Keep sections scannable with short paragraphs and bullet points.
- Use headings that reflect the content directly.

## Review Workflow
1. Draft the content in the manuscript or academy folder.
2. Review for accuracy, clarity, and consistency.
3. Confirm the content aligns with the style guide and branding guide.
4. Move approved content into publishing review or production folders.

## Veterinary and Rescue Notes
- Highlight veterinary awareness where relevant.
- Avoid presenting veterinary advice as a substitute for professional care.
- Include rescue-informed perspectives when appropriate.

## Visual and Publishing Notes
- Pair text with diagrams, checklists, or illustrations where helpful.
- Keep page layouts clean and readable.
- Ensure educational materials remain easy to scan and use.

## Final Review Checklist
- Clear and accurate language
- Consistent formatting
- Appropriate terminology
- Useful sections and summaries
- Ready for review or publication
