# ScalesWiki Principles

## Animal Welfare First

Every recommendation prioritizes the long-term welfare of the animal.

## Evidence-Based

Recommendations are supported by current husbandry knowledge and scientific literature whenever possible.

## Transparent

Sources are cited.

Updates are versioned.

Changes are documented.

## Community Reviewed

Experts are encouraged to review and improve content.

## Education Before Opinion

ScalesWiki explains why recommendations exist instead of asking readers to follow them without context.
