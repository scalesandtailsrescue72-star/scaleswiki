# Water

Fresh, clean water is one of the most basic yet essential requirements for every Ball Python. Water supports hydration, healthy organ function, proper digestion, and successful shedding throughout your snake's life.

Although Ball Pythons do not drink every day, they should always have access to clean drinking water.

---

## Why Water Matters

Water plays an important role in nearly every body system.

Proper hydration helps support:

- Healthy shedding
- Digestion
- Kidney function
- Overall health
- Normal body temperature regulation

Without constant access to clean water, a Ball Python can become dehydrated and experience unnecessary health problems.

---

## Choosing a Water Bowl

Select a sturdy bowl that cannot be tipped over easily.

The bowl should:

- Be large enough for drinking
- Be easy to clean
- Be made from reptile-safe materials
- Remain stable inside the enclosure

Some Ball Pythons will occasionally soak in their water bowl, especially during shedding.

---

## Keeping Water Clean

Water should be checked every day.

Replace the water immediately if it becomes:

- Dirty
- Filled with substrate
- Contaminated with waste

Clean the bowl regularly using reptile-safe cleaning practices before refilling it with fresh water.

---

## Hydration and Shedding

Proper hydration supports healthy skin and complete sheds.

During shedding, many Ball Pythons may drink more frequently or spend additional time inside a humid hide.

Fresh water and proper humidity work together to help support successful shedding.

---

## Signs of Dehydration

Possible signs include:

- Wrinkled skin
- Retained shed
- Sunken eyes
- Lethargy

If dehydration is suspected, review husbandry conditions and consult a qualified reptile veterinarian if necessary.

---

## 🐍 Did You Know?

Ball Pythons often drink during the evening or overnight when they become more active.

---

## 💡 Keeper Tip

Even if the water bowl appears clean, replacing the water daily is one of the easiest ways to promote long-term health.

---

## Key Points

- Fresh water should always be available.
- Water bowls should be cleaned regularly.
- Proper hydration supports healthy shedding.
- A stable, easy-to-clean water bowl is recommended.
- Clean water is a basic part of responsible reptile care.
