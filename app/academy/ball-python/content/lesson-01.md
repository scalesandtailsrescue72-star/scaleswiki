# Introduction

## Welcome to Ball Python 101

Welcome to **ScalesWiki Academy's Ball Python 101**.

This course is designed to help new and experienced keepers develop a strong foundation in responsible ball python husbandry. Rather than relying on isolated tips or social media advice, this handbook presents information in a structured learning format so you can understand **why** each aspect of care matters.

Throughout this course you will learn how to create an appropriate environment, recognize normal behavior, provide proper nutrition, monitor your snake's health, and make informed decisions that support long-term welfare.

---

# What You Will Learn

By completing this course, you will learn how to:

- Understand the natural history of ball pythons and how it influences captive care.
- Choose a healthy snake from a responsible source.
- Design and maintain an appropriate enclosure.
- Provide proper heating, humidity, lighting, and environmental enrichment.
- Feed ball pythons safely throughout different life stages.
- Recognize normal behaviors and identify potential health concerns.
- Practice good hygiene, quarantine, and biosecurity.
- Respond appropriately to common husbandry problems.
- Develop habits that support lifelong responsible reptile keeping.

---

# Who This Course Is For

This handbook is intended for:

- First-time ball python keepers.
- Families considering their first snake.
- Students interested in reptile husbandry.
- Rescue volunteers.
- Educators introducing reptile care.
- Experienced keepers who want a structured review of best practices.

---

# How to Use This Course

Each lesson builds on the previous one.

For the best learning experience:

1. Read each lesson carefully.
2. Study the illustrations and diagrams.
3. Complete the lesson quiz.
4. Review the worksheet if provided.
5. Continue to the next lesson only after understanding the material.

The Final Certification Exam covers information from the entire course, not just individual chapters.

---

# Our Philosophy

At ScalesWiki Academy, responsible reptile keeping begins with education.

Good husbandry is based on observation, preparation, continual learning, and respect for the animal's natural biology. This course emphasizes evidence-informed care, practical decision-making, and animal welfare.

Whenever veterinary care is necessary, professional medical advice should always take precedence over information presented in this handbook.

---

# Animal Welfare Statement

Every recommendation in this course is intended to support the health and welfare of captive ball pythons.

No single guide can replace the expertise of a qualified reptile veterinarian, but a well-informed keeper is better prepared to recognize problems early and provide appropriate daily care.

---

# Certification

Students who complete every lesson and successfully pass the Final Certification Exam will earn a **ScalesWiki Ball Python Certificate of Completion**.

Certification demonstrates successful completion of this educational program. It is not a veterinary credential or professional license.

# Before You Continue

As you work through this handbook, remember that successful reptile keeping is a process of continual learning.

Every chapter adds another piece to the foundation required to provide safe, ethical, and responsible care for ball pythons.

Take your time, ask questions, and focus on understanding the concepts rather than simply memorizing facts.

The goal of this course is not only to help you pass an exam—it is to help you become a confident and responsible keeper.
