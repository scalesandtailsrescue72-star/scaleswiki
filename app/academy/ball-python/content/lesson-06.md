# Humidity

Humidity is one of the most misunderstood aspects of Ball Python husbandry. While these snakes can tolerate small day-to-day changes, consistently incorrect humidity levels can contribute to shedding problems, dehydration, respiratory issues, and unnecessary stress.

Providing proper humidity helps your Ball Python stay healthy throughout its life.

## Why Humidity Matters

Ball Pythons naturally experience seasonal changes in humidity throughout western and central Africa. In captivity, our goal is not to duplicate every weather pattern, but to provide a stable environment that supports normal body functions.

Proper humidity helps with:

- Healthy shedding
- Hydration
- Respiratory health
- Skin condition
- Overall comfort

---

## Recommended Humidity

For most healthy Ball Pythons, aim to maintain humidity between **60–70%**.

During shedding, many keepers find that temporarily increasing humidity to around **70–80%** helps support a complete shed.

Humidity should be measured with a reliable digital hygrometer rather than estimated.

---

## How to Maintain Humidity

Several factors influence enclosure humidity, including:

- Type of substrate
- Size of the water bowl
- Ventilation
- Room humidity
- Enclosure design

If humidity is consistently too low, consider:

- Using a moisture-retaining substrate
- Increasing the surface area of the water bowl
- Lightly misting when appropriate
- Partially covering excessive ventilation
- Providing a humid hide

---

## Humid Hides

A humid hide gives your Ball Python access to higher humidity whenever it needs it.

A typical humid hide contains damp sphagnum moss or another suitable moisture-retaining material.

The hide should remain damp, not soaking wet.

---

## Signs Humidity May Be Too Low

Low humidity may contribute to:

- Incomplete sheds
- Dry skin
- Eye caps remaining after shedding
- Mild dehydration

Address humidity issues promptly rather than attempting to remove retained shed by force.

---

## Signs Humidity May Be Too High

Excessively damp conditions may contribute to:

- Mold growth
- Poor air quality
- Constantly wet substrate
- Increased bacterial growth

Humidity should be balanced with good ventilation.

---

## Measuring Humidity

Always monitor humidity with a quality digital hygrometer placed inside the enclosure.

Avoid guessing based on how the enclosure feels or looks.

Regular monitoring allows you to make small adjustments before problems develop.

---

## 🐍 Did You Know?

Ball Pythons often spend time inside naturally humid burrows and shelters, allowing them to regulate moisture levels even when the surrounding environment is drier.

---

## 💡 Keeper Tip

The goal isn't to make the entire enclosure wet.

Provide appropriate humidity while keeping the enclosure clean, well ventilated, and free from constantly saturated substrate.

---

## Key Points

- Maintain humidity around **60–70%** for most Ball Pythons.
- Increase humidity slightly during shedding if needed.
- Use a digital hygrometer to monitor conditions.
- A humid hide can support healthy sheds.
- Balance humidity with proper ventilation.
