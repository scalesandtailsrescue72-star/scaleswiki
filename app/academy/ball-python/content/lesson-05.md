# Heating

Proper heating is one of the most important aspects of Ball Python husbandry. Because Ball Pythons are ectothermic (cold-blooded), they rely on external heat sources to regulate their body temperature.

Without appropriate temperatures, a Ball Python cannot properly digest food, maintain its immune system, or behave normally.

## Why Heating Matters

In the wild, Ball Pythons move between warmer and cooler areas to regulate their body temperature.

Captive Ball Pythons need the same opportunity by providing a thermal gradient inside the enclosure.

A proper thermal gradient allows your snake to choose the temperature it needs at any given time.

---

## Warm Side

The warm side of the enclosure should provide a basking area of approximately **88–92°F (31–33°C)**.

This warm area supports:

- Digestion
- Immune function
- Normal activity
- Proper metabolism

---

## Cool Side

The opposite side of the enclosure should remain around **76–80°F (24–27°C)**.

This cooler area allows your Ball Python to avoid overheating while still remaining comfortable.

---

## Measuring Temperature

Never guess enclosure temperatures.

Use:

- Digital thermometers
- Infrared temperature gun (optional)
- Reliable thermostat

Avoid relying on stick-on dial thermometers, as they often provide inaccurate readings.

---

## Thermostats

Every heat source should be controlled by a thermostat.

A thermostat helps:

- Prevent overheating
- Protect your Ball Python
- Extend equipment life
- Maintain stable temperatures

Running heating equipment without a thermostat can become dangerous.

---

## Heat Sources

Common heating options include:

- Overhead radiant heat
- Deep Heat Projectors
- Ceramic Heat Emitters
- Halogen heat lamps (when appropriate)

Choose equipment that safely provides consistent temperatures for your enclosure size.

---

## Signs Temperatures May Be Incorrect

Temperatures that are too low may contribute to:

- Poor appetite
- Difficulty digesting food
- Lethargy
- Increased susceptibility to illness

Temperatures that are too high may cause:

- Constant hiding
- Excessive soaking
- Stress
- Dehydration

---

## 🐍 Did You Know?

Ball Pythons don't simply need "heat." They need the ability to choose different temperatures throughout the day, just as they would in nature.

---

## 💡 Keeper Tip

Always verify temperatures using a reliable digital thermometer before introducing your Ball Python into a new enclosure.

Small temperature differences can have a significant impact on your snake's health.

---

## Key Points

- Ball Pythons require a thermal gradient.
- Use digital thermometers to monitor temperatures.
- Every heat source should be controlled by a thermostat.
- Stable temperatures are essential for digestion and overall health.
