# ScalesWiki Veterinary Review Outreach Emails

## Purpose

These emails are designed to respectfully invite veterinary professionals to review ScalesWiki educational materials.

The goal is professional feedback, not veterinary endorsement.

---

# Email 1 — Initial Veterinary Review Invitation

## Subject:

Invitation to Review ScalesWiki Ball Python 101 Educational Materials

---

Hello Dr. [Last Name],

My name is William Pierce, and I am the founder of ScalesWiki, a rescue-backed reptile education project focused on creating responsible, accessible husbandry resources.

We have recently completed our first educational course:

**ScalesWiki Academy — Ball Python 101**

Before wider promotion, we are seeking feedback from veterinary professionals to help improve the accuracy, clarity, and safety of our educational materials.

The course includes:

- Structured husbandry lessons
- Environmental care guidance
- Knowledge checks
- Final certification assessment
- Educational resources for reptile keepers

We are not seeking veterinary endorsement or sponsorship. We are requesting professional feedback that can help ensure the information we provide is responsible, accurate, and useful for reptile keepers.

[Veterinary Review Link]

Thank you for considering helping improve reptile education.

# Email 2 — Founding Veterinary Reviewer Invitation

## Subject:

Invitation to Become a Founding Veterinary Reviewer for ScalesWiki

---

Hello Dr. [Last Name],

Thank you for your interest in ScalesWiki.

As we continue developing our reptile education resources, we are establishing a small group of veterinary professionals who will help provide guidance during our review process.

We would like to invite you to participate as a:

**Founding Veterinary Reviewer**

Founding reviewers may contribute by:

- Reviewing educational chapters
- Providing accuracy feedback
- Identifying areas needing clarification

Participation represents professional feedback and educational contribution. It does not represent veterinary endorsement, sponsorship, or responsibility for final educational materials.
