# Editorial Guidelines

## Our Principles

Evidence-informed.

Clear.

Honest.

Educational.

---

## Every chapter should answer:

1. What is it?

2. Why does it matter?

3. How does it affect captive husbandry?

---

## Writing Style

- Write for beginners without talking down to experienced keepers.
- Explain scientific concepts in plain language.
- Distinguish established evidence from expert opinion when research is limited.
- Cite reliable sources whenever possible.
