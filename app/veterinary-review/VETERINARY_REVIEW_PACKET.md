# Introduction

Thank you for considering reviewing ScalesWiki educational materials.

ScalesWiki is a rescue-backed reptile education platform created to provide accessible, responsible husbandry information for reptile keepers, adopters, and rescue communities.

Our goal is to create educational resources that encourage responsible ownership, improve animal welfare, and help keepers understand the needs of their animals.

Before public promotion of our certification courses, we are seeking feedback from veterinary professionals to help improve accuracy, clarity, and safety.

---

# Current Review Project

## Ball Python 101 Certification Course

**Course Status:** Veterinary Review Draft  
**Version:** 1.0

The Ball Python 101 course is designed to introduce keepers to responsible Ball Python care.

The course includes:

- Natural history and species background
- Choosing and preparing for a Ball Python
- Enclosure design
- Temperature management
- Humidity management
- Feeding practices
- Handling and enrichment
- Health awareness
- Long-term husbandry

The course includes:

- Structured educational lessons
- Knowledge checks
- Final certification examination
- Printable educational resources
