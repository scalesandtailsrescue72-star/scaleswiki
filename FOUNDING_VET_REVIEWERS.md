# ScalesWiki Founding Veterinary Reviewers

This document recognizes veterinary professionals who contribute their time and expertise to improving the accuracy, safety, and educational quality of the ScalesWiki Professional Field Guide Series.

Founding Veterinary Reviewers help provide professional feedback during the development and revision process of ScalesWiki educational materials.

---

# Purpose of the Program

The Founding Veterinary Reviewer Program exists to:

- Improve the accuracy of reptile education resources
- Identify areas where information can be clarified
- Strengthen animal welfare and safety messaging
- Help create responsible educational materials for reptile keepers and rescue communities

---

# Reviewer Contribution

Veterinary reviewers may contribute through:

- Reviewing educational chapters
- Providing husbandry feedback
- Identifying areas needing clarification
- Suggesting improvements
- Reviewing health-related wording and safety information

---

Each contributing reviewer may be recognized with:

# Review Status Definitions

**Invited**  
Reviewer has been contacted and invited to participate.

**Reviewing**  
Reviewer is actively reviewing ScalesWiki materials.

**Completed Review**  
Reviewer has completed feedback for a specific resource or chapter.

**Acknowledged Contributor**  
Reviewer has approved public acknowledgment of their contribution.

---

# Important Note

Participation as a reviewer represents professional feedback and educational contribution. It does not represent veterinary endorsement, sponsorship, or responsibility for the final educational material.
