# ScalesWiki Branding Guide

## Brand Identity
ScalesWiki is a professional, educational, and conservation-minded brand focused on reptile care, rescue awareness, and field-guide publishing.

## Visual Direction
- Modern and trustworthy
- Nature-inspired and scientific
- Clean, readable, and editorial
- Warm but professional

## Primary Color
- Forest Green
- Use for primary actions, major brand surfaces, and key emphasis.

## Secondary Color
- Slate Gray
- Use for supporting UI, muted text, and neutral sections.

## Accent Color
- Gold
- Use sparingly for highlights, callouts, and premium emphasis.

## Danger Color
- Deep Red
- Use for warnings, alerts, and urgent calls to action.

## Typography
- Headings: Libre Baskerville
- Body: Inter
- Code: JetBrains Mono

## Logo Usage
- Preserve clear space around the logo.
- Use the full logo on primary brand surfaces.
- Use the icon mark alone when space is limited.
- Avoid stretching, skewing, or placing the logo on busy backgrounds.

## Minimum Spacing
- Keep a minimum clear space equal to the height of the logo mark around the logo.
- Maintain consistent spacing in web, print, and social assets.

## Icon Standards
- Use simple, geometric icons with a consistent stroke width.
- Keep icons legible at small sizes.
- Match icon style to the brand's modern editorial feel.

## Image Style
- Favor clean, high-contrast imagery with a natural palette.
- Use photography and illustrations that feel educational and trustworthy.
- Keep compositions uncluttered and focused on the subject.

## Illustration Standards
- Use line work and color that support clarity and readability.
- Prefer informative diagrams, anatomical diagrams, and habitat illustrations.
- Keep illustrations aligned with the brand's scientific and educational tone.

## Photography Standards
- Use natural lighting whenever possible.
- Emphasize habitat, close-up detail, and keeper context.
- Maintain a calm, professional, and respectful presentation.

## Brand Applications
- Website hero and navigation
- Manuscript cover and chapter headers
- Academy materials and social graphics
- Publishing templates and print assets
