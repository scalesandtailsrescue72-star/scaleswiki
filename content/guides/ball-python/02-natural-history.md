# Natural History

Understanding where Ball Pythons come from is the foundation of good husbandry. Every recommendation you'll learn later in this course—from enclosure design to heating and humidity—is based on how these snakes live in the wild.

## Native Range

Ball Pythons (*Python regius*) are native to western and central Africa. Their range includes countries such as:

- Ghana
- Togo
- Benin
- Nigeria
- Cameroon
- Ivory Coast
- Parts of Chad and neighboring regions

Although their distribution is broad, they occupy habitats with similar environmental conditions.

## Natural Habitat

Ball Pythons are primarily terrestrial snakes that spend much of their lives hidden beneath vegetation or inside secure shelters.

Common habitats include:

- Tropical savannas
- Grasslands
- Forest edges
- Agricultural fields
- Abandoned rodent burrows
- Termite mounds

These protected hiding places help regulate temperature, conserve moisture, and provide protection from predators.

## Climate

The regions where Ball Pythons naturally occur experience warm temperatures throughout the year with seasonal changes in rainfall.

Typical conditions include:

- Warm daytime temperatures
- Slightly cooler nights
- A rainy season with increased humidity
- A drier season with lower humidity

These natural conditions explain why captive Ball Pythons require both proper heating and carefully managed humidity.

## Daily Behavior

Ball Pythons are primarily crepuscular to nocturnal, meaning they are most active around dusk, overnight, and near dawn.

During daylight hours they usually remain hidden inside secure shelters to avoid predators and excessive heat.

Rather than chasing prey, Ball Pythons are ambush predators. They wait patiently for small mammals to pass before striking quickly and constricting their prey.

## Why Natural History Matters

Understanding how Ball Pythons live in nature helps explain nearly every husbandry recommendation you'll encounter.

Their natural habitat teaches us why they need:

- Warm and cool temperature zones
- Secure hiding places
- Appropriate humidity
- Clean water
- Opportunities to explore and climb
- Low-stress environments

Successful reptile keeping isn't about guessing—it's about recreating the conditions these animals evolved to live in.

---

## 🐍 Did You Know?

Despite their reputation as ground-dwelling snakes, Ball Pythons are capable climbers and may climb shrubs or low trees while exploring or searching for food.

---

## 💡 Keeper Tip

Whenever you're unsure about a husbandry decision, ask yourself:

**"What would this snake experience in nature?"**

Using the species' natural history as your guide is one of the best ways to provide appropriate care.

---

## Key Points

- Ball Pythons are native to western and central Africa.
- They naturally inhabit grasslands, savannas, and forest edges.
- Their activity patterns are primarily crepuscular to nocturnal.
- They are ambush predators.
- Proper husbandry is based on recreating their natural environment.
